i'm not getting reminders

on iPhone, go to settings → notifications → life is calling and turn on allow notifications, sounds and time sensitive. then open the app once. in the app, swipe to the life panel and tap "send a test notification".

cancel or manage premium

on iPhone: settings → your name → subscriptions → life is calling. cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. on android: play store → profile → payments & subscriptions → subscriptions.

restore premium on a new phone

open the paywall in the app (tap "get premium" in the life panel) and tap "restore purchases". use the same apple id or google account you bought it with.

refunds

payments go through apple or google, so refunds do too. on iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
