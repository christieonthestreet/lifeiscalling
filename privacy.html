privacy policy

last updated 10 november 2026

the short version

  • no account, no sign-up, no email needed to use the app.
  • your name, your life numbers and your diary stay on your phone.
  • no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • if you buy premium, apple or google handles the payment. we never see your card.

who we are

life is calling is made by Christie Aucamp-Schutte, a sole trader in Sydney, Australia. "we" and "us" in this policy means her.

what stays on your phone

everything you type into the app is saved only on your device: the name you give it, your birthday and the life estimate used for your counters, your diary entries, and your settings. we have no server that receives any of this, so we can't read it. reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. if you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

purchases

premium is paid through apple or google, under their own privacy policies (apple, google). we never receive your name, email or card details.

to know whether you have premium, the app uses RevenueCat. it receives a random id created by the app (not linked to your name or email), your purchase history from the store, your device type and app version, and your country. it uses this only to unlock premium and restore it on a new phone.

emails and beta testing

if you join the beta or our email list, we keep your email address to send you the beta and updates about the app, and nothing else. you can unsubscribe or ask us to delete it at any time.

what we don't do

  • no advertising or ad networks.
  • no analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • no selling or sharing of personal information.

children

life is calling is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

your rights

your app content lives only on your device, so you control it completely: edit or delete it in the app, or delete the app to remove it all. for any privacy question or request, including under the Australian Privacy Act or the GDPR, email christieonthestreet@gmail.com.

changes

if this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. if the app starts collecting something new, we'll tell you in the app first.
